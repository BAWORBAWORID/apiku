02 — Security
Built with security first.
Multi-layer protection to keep your requests safe and your data private.

SSRF Protection

Centralized middleware blocks requests to private IPs, DNS rebinding, file://, and gopher:// protocols.

IP Spoofing Prevention

Smart IP detection only trusts Cloudflare edge headers. Spoofed cf-connecting-ip and X-Forwarded-For are ignored.

Bot Shield Firewall

AI-powered firewall auto-blocks scanners, probes, and malicious traffic before reaching your endpoints.

Smart Rate Limiting

Configurable per-IP rate limits with auto-ban. Global lock activates on DDoS detection with Telegram alerts.
